# gcal-client

A native desktop client for Google Calendar that shows the calendars of several Google
accounts in one view. Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), for Linux.

- [Download and instructions](https://github.com/vsisl/gcal-client#readme)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.md)
- [Source code](https://github.com/vsisl/gcal-client)

It talks to Google and to nothing else: no statistics, no crash reports, no update checks.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Google Calendar is a trademark of Google LLC.
